# clinic-ops

clinic-ops models the outpatient operations of a Japanese hospital: reception with online
eligibility checks, the consultation, billing at the window after the visit, in-house and
outside prescriptions, and monthly claim statements to the review organizations.

## Sources of the business rules

The `//` comments in `shared/*.rdra` and `buc/*.rdra` cite a source as `<file>.txt:<line>`.
The files are copies of primary sources (e-Gov law texts, MHLW and SSK documents, public
hospital specifications) saved during the research on 2026-10-29. They are not in this
repository, because the copies are for reading and are not redistributed.

- The copies live in `~/research/japan-hospital-operations/sources/` on the machine where
  the research ran.
- `~/research/japan-hospital-operations/map.md` maps each file to its original URL in the
  table `原文（sources/）`.
- A line number refers to the saved copy, not to the page at the original URL. To follow a
  citation elsewhere, fetch the original URL and search for the quoted words.

`design-sample.md` is generated by `scripts/gen-clinic-ops-design-sample.py`.
